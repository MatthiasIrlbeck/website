### Question and model

A random tessellation provides a geometric setting for percolation: cells receive random states, and connected regions can be studied through those states. This entry concerns Poisson-Voronoi percolation in high dimensions.

The notation $d$ refers to the dimension in a general Euclidean model. A finite simulation can show a tessellation and its colouring, but does not by itself explain the behaviour of an infinite system or establish a high-dimensional theorem.
