### Question and model

Percolation asks how local random choices can produce large connected structures. In Poisson-Voronoi percolation, the underlying geometry is a random tessellation. This entry concerns a model in hyperbolic space and the question of uniqueness.

For an illustrative percolation model, a standard quantity is the probability that a chosen origin belongs to an infinite cluster:

$$
\theta(p) = \mathbb{P}_p\bigl(|C(o)| = \infty\bigr).
$$

Here $p$ is a model parameter and $C(o)$ denotes the cluster of the origin. This formula is a model-definition and typesetting example; it is not the paper’s theorem or a complete definition of its hyperbolic model.
