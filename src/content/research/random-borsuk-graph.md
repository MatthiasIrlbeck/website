### Question and model

The title points to a threshold question for colouring a random Borsuk graph. A colouring assigns labels to vertices so that adjacent vertices receive different labels. The number of colours needed depends on the edges, and in a random geometric model those edges reflect both geometry and randomness.

As a general graph-theoretic definition, for a graph $G=(V,E)$,

$$
\chi(G) = \min\{k : \exists c\colon V \to \{1,\ldots,k\},\ c(u) \neq c(v)\ \text{for every } \{u,v\}\in E\}.
$$

This is a background definition, not a result of this paper. The precise sampling model and threshold parameter need an author-approved explanation.
