### Question and model

A Voronoi tessellation assigns each location to its nearest point in a collection of sites. When those sites form a Poisson point process, the resulting cells are random. This paper concerns the shape of a typical cell in high dimensions.

For typesetting and model illustration only, a cell around a site $x$ can be described by

$$
V(x) = \{y \in \mathbb{R}^d : \|y-x\| \leq \|y-z\| \text{ for every other site } z\}.
$$

This standard definition is background, not a statement of the paper’s results. The precise notion of a typical cell, the scaling, and the quantities studied should be described using author-approved text.
